Privacy policy: YT Uploader

Last updated: 22 October 2026

YT Uploader is a private tool used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels (Marblephonic and Signpost). It is not offered to other users.

What it accesses

Through Google's OAuth sign-in and the YouTube Data, Analytics and Reporting APIs, it accesses only the owner's channels: uploading and editing videos, thumbnails and channel details; reading channel and video analytics; and reading and replying to comments on those channels' videos.

How data is stored and used

Sign-in tokens and the analytics it reads are stored only on the owner's own computer, used only to run and improve these channels, and never sold or shared with third parties. Public comment text may be read to understand viewer interest; it is not stored beyond what is needed for that.

Google API Services

YT Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google account permissions. Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
